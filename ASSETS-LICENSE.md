# Pawmail artwork, characters and writing — all rights reserved

The source code of Pawmail is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). **The things listed
below are not.** They are what make Pawmail look and read like Pawmail, and all
rights to them are reserved by The Pawmail Authors.

In Pawmail the artwork is drawn by code, so some of it lives in source files.
Those files are part of this list, even though they are JavaScript.

**Characters and artwork**

- The look of the pets (the cat, including the lucky-cat coat, the panda, the capybara, the Shiba Inu, the red panda, the bunny,
  the fox with its scarf, the penguin and the otter) and the things that turn up on them (the capybara's orange, the snacks and toys):
  `src/renderer/js/pet/species/`, `src/renderer/js/pet/props.js`
- The clothing: `src/renderer/js/pet/outfits.js`
- The festival costumes: `src/renderer/js/pet/looks.js`
- The paper-ball "food": `src/renderer/js/pet/food.js`
- The letter scenery and postcard design: `src/renderer/js/mail/scenes.js`, `src/renderer/js/mail/postcard.js`
- The gift icons: `src/renderer/js/mail/gifts.js`
- The app icon and every image in `build/` and `docs/`

**Writing**

- Every letter, place, event, gift name and festival letter:
  `src/shared/letters/`, `src/shared/festivals/zh.json`, `src/shared/festivals/en.json`
- Clothing names and lines: `src/shared/outfits/zh.json`, `src/shared/outfits/en.json`
- The pet's speech and the interface text: `src/shared/locales/`

**Names**

- The names "Pawmail" and "爪爪来信".

## What you may do

- Download, play, and share the free builds we publish, unchanged.
- Take screenshots and recordings of Pawmail and post them, write about it, stream it.
- Read the code, learn from it, and use the MIT-licensed parts in your own projects.
- Fork the repository to fix bugs or suggest changes back to us.

## What you may not do without asking

- Sell Pawmail or a modified copy of it, or publish it under another name.
- Reuse the pets, clothing, costumes, scenery or writing in your own project.

If you would like to do something that isn't covered here, please ask by opening
an issue on GitHub.
