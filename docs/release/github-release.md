## Title

Pawmail 0.3.0 beta · 爪爪来信 0.3.0 测试版

## Notes

**What's new**

- **Four new pets: a bunny, a fox, an otter and a penguin**, so there are nine to choose from. The bunny hops everywhere and jumps the highest; the fox is curious and bouncy and always wears its red scarf; the otter is clingy and comes looking for you more often; the penguin waddles, can't climb walls, and hops onto low windows. The *Animal* row of the right-click menu now shows them in two rows.
- **Three signature tricks for every pet.** The cat kneads, chases a ball of yarn and squeezes into a box; the panda rolls over and dozes off; the fox dives head-first into the "snow"; the penguin belly-slides across your desktop; the otter juggles pebbles… and everyone pulls out a favorite snack now and then. To see one right away: right-click → *Do a trick!*
- **A little game for each pet**, started from its right-click menu: tease the cat with a feather wand, throw the ball for the Shiba yourself, let the fox pounce on a "mouse" in the snow (wiggle your cursor along the ground), hold out a branch for the red panda to ride, have a bamboo tug-of-war with the panda, set up hurdles for the bunny, stack oranges on the capybara's head, hold the otter's paw, and toss fish for the penguin to catch in mid-air.
- **To-dos.** Write them in the letter window (right-click → *To-dos*). When one is due, the pet runs over holding a sign, and a Windows notification pops up; you can also get a heads-up a few minutes, an hour or a day before.
  **Optional phone reminders** through ntfy or ServerChan. This is the only part of Pawmail that goes online. It's off by default, and when you turn it on it only sends the reminder to the service you picked (you can leave out what the to-do says).
- **The wardrobe now has 50 items** (34 new hats, glasses, tops, bags and things to hold), still delivered with its letters.
- After 12:30 a.m. it brings a little pillow and tells you to go to bed (at most three times a night). On a laptop running low on battery, it gets sleepy too.
- The gift icons are now drawn by hand in code instead of emoji.

**Download one of these:**

- `Pawmail-Setup-0.3.0.exe`: installer (installs for your account, no admin needed)
- `Pawmail-0.3.0-win-x64.zip`: no install; unzip and run `Pawmail.exe`

**Updating from 0.2.0:** quit the running pet first (right-click it → *Quit Pawmail*), then run the new installer, or unzip over the old folder. Your letters, gifts and clothes carry over.

Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit. The first time you run it, Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info → Run anyway**. Pawmail isn't code-signed yet.

Found a bug? Please [open an issue](../../issues) and attach `pawmail.log` (tray icon → *Help & feedback → Open log folder*).

**新内容**

- **四只新动物：兔子、狐狸、水獭、企鹅**，现在一共九只可以选。兔子走路一蹦一蹦的，跳得最高；狐狸机灵好奇、爱蹦跳，脖子上一直围着红围巾；水獭黏人，比别的动物更常跑来找你；企鹅走路左摇右摆，不会爬墙，但能跳上矮矮的窗口。右键菜单的"动物"一行改成了两行。
- **每只动物三个招牌动作。** 猫会踩奶、拍毛线球、钻纸箱；熊猫会打滚、打瞌睡歪倒；狐狸会一头扎进"雪"里；企鹅会肚皮着地在桌面上滑出去；水獭会抛小石子玩杂耍……大家还会时不时掏出自己爱吃的零食。想马上看一个：右键 → *来一个！*
- **每只动物一个陪它玩的小游戏**，在右键菜单里开始：拿逗猫棒逗猫，自己扔球给柴犬追，陪狐狸扑雪地里的"老鼠"（鼠标贴着地面晃），给小熊猫递一根树枝让它趴上去荡，跟熊猫抢竹子，给兔子摆小栏杆跳，往卡皮巴拉头顶叠橘子，牵水獭的手，抛小鱼给企鹅在空中接。
- **待办。** 在信纸窗口里写（右键 → *待办*）。到点了它会举着小牌子跑来提醒你，同时弹一条 Windows 通知；还可以提前几分钟、一小时或一天先提醒一次。
  **手机提醒（可选）**：通过 ntfy 或 Server酱 发到手机上。这是爪爪来信里唯一会联网的功能，默认关着；打开以后也只把提醒发给你选的那一家服务（可以选择不发待办的具体内容）。
- **衣柜扩充到 50 件**（新增帽子、眼镜、上衣、包包、手里拿的东西共 34 件），照样随信寄来。
- 凌晨 0:30 以后你还在用电脑，它会抱着小枕头来劝你睡觉（一晚最多三次）。笔记本电量低时，它也会没精神。
- 礼物图标换成了用代码画的原创图标，不再用表情符号。

**二选一下载：**

- `Pawmail-Setup-0.3.0.exe`：安装程序（只装给你自己，不需要管理员权限）
- `Pawmail-0.3.0-win-x64.zip`：免安装，解压后运行 `Pawmail.exe`

**从 0.2.0 更新：** 先退出正在运行的它（右键 → *退出爪爪来信*），再运行新的安装程序，或者把压缩包解压覆盖原来的文件夹。信、礼物和衣服都还在。

支持 Windows 10 / 11（64 位）。第一次运行时 Windows 可能提示"Windows 已保护你的电脑"，点 **更多信息 → 仍要运行**（暂时还没有代码签名）。

发现问题？欢迎[提 issue](../../issues)，附上 `pawmail.log` 会很有帮助（托盘图标 → *帮助与反馈 → 打开日志文件夹*）。
