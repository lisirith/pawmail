## Description（正文，英文在前、中文在后）

**A tiny pet that lives on your Windows desktop.** It wanders along your taskbar, naps, chases your cursor and climbs the edges of the screen. While you're away it goes exploring, and the next day it greets you with a letter about where it went, sometimes with a little gift enclosed.

**What's in this beta**

- Five pets to choose from: an orange cat (with a lucky-cat coat), a panda, a capybara, a Shiba Inu and a red panda, drawn and animated in code. They breathe, blink, follow your cursor and decide for themselves what to do, and each has its own temper
- A letter every day: 21 places, little stories, a line about your day, and 38 gifts to collect (plus festival-only ones). Save any letter as a postcard to share
- A wardrobe of 50 hats, glasses, tops, bags and things to hold, delivered with its letters
- Pick it up by the scruff and swing it around; drag files onto it and it eats them (they go to the Recycle Bin, so you can get them back)
- It perches on your windows: drop it on one, or watch it hop up or climb a window's side by itself, then ride along when you drag the window
- Birthdays and festivals from China and the West, monthly letters and a year in review
- Hides by itself during full-screen games and videos
- English and Chinese

**Install:** download the installer or the zip. The first time you run it, Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (Pawmail isn't code-signed yet).

**Privacy:** everything stays on your computer. Pawmail never connects to the internet, and it never records your keystrokes, programs or websites.

Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit. Free. This is a beta, so please tell us about anything odd in the comments.

**一只住在你 Windows 桌面上的小动物。** 它在任务栏上走来走去、打瞌睡、追你的鼠标、扒着屏幕边往上爬。你不在的时候它会出门转转，第二天抱着一封信等你拆——讲讲它去了哪儿，有时还随信附上一件小礼物。

**这个测试版有：**

- 五只动物任你选：橘猫（还有招财猫花色）、熊猫、卡皮巴拉、柴犬、小熊猫，全部用代码画、用代码动：会呼吸、眨眼、看你的鼠标，自己决定做什么，各有各的脾气
- 每天一封信：21 个地方、在那儿遇到的小事、一句关于你的话，还有 38 件礼物可以收集（另有节日限定）。每封信都能存成明信片分享
- 衣柜：帽子、眼镜、上衣、包包、手里拿的东西共 50 件，随信寄来
- 拎住后脖子提起来晃一晃；把文件拖给它吃（其实是放进回收站，能找回来）
- 站到你的窗口上：你把它放上去，或者它自己跳上去、顺着窗口侧边爬上去；拖动窗口，它跟着一起走
- 生日和中西方节日，每月一封月报，元旦一封年度回顾
- 全屏打游戏、看视频时自己躲起来
- 中文 / English

**安装：** 下载安装程序或压缩包。第一次运行时，Windows 可能提示"Windows 已保护你的电脑"，点 **更多信息**，再点 **仍要运行**（爪爪来信暂时还没有代码签名）。

**隐私：** 所有数据只存在你的电脑上。爪爪来信从不联网，也不记录你的按键、程序或网页。

支持 Windows 10 / 11（64 位）。免费。这是测试版，遇到奇怪的地方欢迎在评论里告诉我们。
